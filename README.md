# Moon Chess

Moon Chess is a cool take on tic tac toe that i saw while playing the hit game, Genshin Impact where it is just normal tic tac toe, but you can only have 3 signs at the table at once,
your oldest tile will get destroyed when you put a 3rd one. I just though it was cool and wanted to make it myself again and even upgrade it a little bit more at some point.

I did also wanna learn some ratatui so its nice for that aswell.
